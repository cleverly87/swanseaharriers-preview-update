Milestones

  • 1962 Swansea Harriers formed from Cockett AC, the athletics section of Cockett Boys Club.
  • 1970 Men win the Welsh Men's League and enter British League Division 4.
  • 1994 Men win the 12-stage national road relay, the first Welsh club to take the title (also road relay wins in 1988 and 1991).
  • 2006 New outdoor track and field facility opens at Swansea University.
  • 2009 · 2014 Named UK Athletics Club of the Year, twice.
  • 2011 Dai Greene becomes 400m hurdles world champion in Daegu.
  • 2015 Women become the first non-English club to win the ERRA 4-stage road relay.
  • 2017 Men promoted to the top tier of the British League; membership peaks at 766.
  • 2018 The club becomes a registered charity.
  • 2025 Men and women promoted to the National Athletics League Premier Division for 2026.
