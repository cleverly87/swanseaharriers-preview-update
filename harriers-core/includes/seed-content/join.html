How to join in three steps

  1. Come and train. Try a session with the right group for your age and event. Off-track runners can train twice before joining.
  2. Request a form. Email the membership secretary for a club membership form.
  3. Pay and pull on the vest. Pay the joining fee and you are registered with the club and Welsh Athletics.

Questions parents ask

How old does my child need to be?

Club membership starts from school year 5. Younger children can join our satellite sessions or Sunday junior parkrun.

Do we pay per session as well?

Development and track sessions are £3.60 each, contactless only, paid on arrival at the club café. Most off-track sessions are free.

Does my child have to compete?

Yes. We are a competitive club, so members are expected to compete for Swansea Harriers when selected.

Still unsure?

Come to a session first. Our coaches will point you to the right group.
